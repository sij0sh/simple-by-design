# Semantic boundaries

A gap needs a concrete witness:

- **Pair:** two outcomes consistent with everything established, materially distinguished by requester, criterion, or constraint.
- **Defeater:** a relied-on claim plus a plausible, unexcluded condition under which it fails and changes the outcome. For criteria, try an outcome that passes but misses the goal—including doing nothing. For a choice, try the strongest viable rival.
- **Failed check:** a required design criterion whose evidence is missing or contradicted. This is not a license to invent requirements.

No witness: no gap. Confidence is not evidence. Cite request words, path:lines, documents, observations, or established decisions for relied-on claims. Distinguish observation from inference.

## Resolver separation

Never settle a gap with another type's resolver.

- **Intent**—wanted, allowed, valued: requester or authoritative requester documents. Ask; never pick. Project convention is not requester authorization.
- **Fact**—true of system/world: cheapest authorized observation whose result changes the design. A plausible cause or model is not an observed fact.
- **Choice**—which viable option: first source that answers:
  1. Requirements and adopted, established decisions; verify assumptions first.
  2. Project conventions; replace one only by explicit decision.
  3. `defaults.md`.
  4. `contract.md`, applicable existing language guidance, instruction-quality guidance below.
  5. Simplest adequate choice.

The CLI checks resolver/provenance shapes, not source authenticity, completeness, or whether the highest applicable policy was used. Those remain agent/reviewer responsibilities. Do not manufacture a language-reference catalog.

## Three-way gate

1. Nobody treats the outcomes differently: implementation freedom, no question needed.
2. Fact/choice whose wrong pick is cheap to undo and would be noticed: explicitly provisional; cite inferred basis, reversal, detection, and revisit condition; proceed.
3. Otherwise settle now. If resolver unavailable, state what/who is needed and pause. Build nothing irreversible past it. Never use provisional intent.

Deferral is not a resolution. It needs a requester-sourced boundary outside the present design, owner, reason, and trigger; a required current-design question cannot be deferred away.

Reopen affected work when observation contradicts a relied-on claim, you catch yourself choosing outcomes the requester would distinguish, a criterion becomes uncheckable, or a revisit condition holds. Invalidate dependent conclusions, designs, checks, and completion; preserve history.

## Instruction choices

Add only the smallest behavioral contract that changes a specific decision under identifiable conditions toward a verifiable result. Check necessity, observability, scope, freedom, conflict, and risk. Remove an instruction when its absence changes no consequential behavior. Do not prescribe a method where the outcome suffices.
